Measurements and other values you enter—such as length, width, height, diameter, quantity, unit selections, price, and calculated concrete volume or cost—are processed directly on your device. The Application does not send these calculation inputs or results to a server operated by the Service Provider.

Purpose of the Application

The Application is a concrete quantity and cost estimation tool intended for informational and educational purposes. It supports calculations for items such as slabs, walls, square or round footings, columns, holes, tubes, curbs, gutters, barriers, and stairs.
